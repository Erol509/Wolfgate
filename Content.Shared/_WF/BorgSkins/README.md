# BorgSkins

Tall (32x64), wide (64x32) and large (64x64) cyborg skins from Bubberstation: the Meka, NiKA, NiKO, K4T and Dullahan
tallborgs, the drake, vale, hound, borgi, otie and other quadruped borgs, the K1-T7Y and C9-T7O cat borgs, the Myomer
and the Wyvern. Each is a cosmetic `borgSubtype`, picked in the borg's type menu next to the existing skins. Skins for
departments with no borg type here (cargo, security, peacekeeper, ninja, syndicate, clown, research, Tarkon) are
offered under the generic type.

Entry points: `WFBorgSkinPrototype` (per-subtype offset and animation flags, same ID as the subtype) and the client
`WFBorgSkinSystem`, which lifts tall sprites onto their tile, keeps the eye and lamp layers in step with an animated
body and swaps to the `_moving` states while the borg has speed.

## Sprites

`Tools/_WF/BorgSkins/convert.py <dmi folder> <Bubberstation commit>` rebuilds every RSI, `subtypes.yml`,
`chassis.yml` and `chassis.ftl` from Bubberstation's `modular_skyrat/modules/borgs/icons/{tallrobot,widerobot}*.dmi`
and, in a `zubbers` subfolder, `modular_zubbers/code/modules/silicons/borgs/sprites` (`tallrobot*.dmi` for the
dullahans and the `kittycatborgs`, `myomer` and `wyvern` folders); its skin table decides which icon state becomes
which skin. States are renamed to what the skin's borg type looks up (`engineer`, `engineer_e`, `engineer_e_r`,
`engineer_l`, ...).

The skyrat art is CC-BY-SA 3.0. The tallborg artist asks that the skinline is ported in full and that the readme
(`Resources/Textures/_WF/BorgSkins/Tall/readme.md`) and the `credit` state stay with it, so every department is
converted even where nothing uses it. Each RSI also keeps the `rest`, `sit`, `bellyup`, `wreck` and `tipped` poses,
and each sheet's leftover states (cover and weapon overlays, sleeper lights) are in an `extras.rsi`; no code uses
those yet.

The dullahans are by Toriate and Cecily, who agreed to the port. Only the dullahan states are taken from the zubbers
`tallrobot` sheets; the other skins in them are not ported.

The cat borgs, Myomer and Wyvern are by Toriate and are CC BY-NC-SA 3.0: non-commercial, unlike the rest. Their
`CREDIT` and `LICENSE` files are kept in `Resources/Textures/_WF/BorgSkins/Credits`.
