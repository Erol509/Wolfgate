# BulkCargo

Buy crates in bulk from a station's trade vendors instead of vending them one at a time. A cart of several products is
priced crate by crate down the market's own shelf curve (`WGTradeMarketSystem.CartQuote`), paid from the buyer's bank as
one transaction, and delivered a crate at a time into a rectangular zone that two placed markers define. Every tile takes
one crate, and a zone is saved in grid-local tiles so it follows a ship as it moves. Several products can share one cart,
and several queued orders can share one zone, while their reserved tiles fit; a new zone may not overlap an existing one.

The cart pricing and stock primitives live in the TradeGoods module (`WGTradeMarketSystem.Bulk.cs`), since they are the
market's own curve. This module owns the rest: the zone corners and their cell classification and reservations
(`WGCargoDeliveryZoneSystem`), the deployable projector that places a corner at range and spends itself on the pair
(`WGCargoZoneProjectorSystem`), the quoted cart and the paid order (`WGCargoBulkOrderSystem`), the delivery queue that
sends one crate per interval into its reserved tile (`WGCargoBulkOrderSystem.Delivery.cs`), and the marker crate that
tells the market a delivered crate was already taken off the shelf (`WGBulkPurchasedComponent`). `WGCargoBulkVendorComponent`
tunes a vendor's interval, blocked timeout and arrival effect; the trade vendor prototypes carry it.

A vendor's bulk screen is the existing vending machine: the same machine, stock and prices, with a cart and a delivery
zone picker. The marked bay itself shows a pass-through orange energy field in the world, drawn as one rippling overlay
pass per zone, with animated L-shaped corner markers and edge decals around it. The markers are one drawn north-west
corner turned by the server to face into the rectangle; the zone projector uses the NFSD holoprojector sprite. Its second
tab is a live ship plot: the hull's floor, walls and doors drawn as an orange blueprint, the chosen bay boxed in amber,
and the paid order's crates filling in one at a time. It may deliver to its own station's zones and to zones on ships
whose hull lies within reach of the counter, measured from the hull so a long hauler docked at the near end still
qualifies; a berth on the far side of a station is not enough. An order accepted for a ship keeps to that ship's grid
after undocking.

The screen always offers the counter as a destination too. That mode has no markers: it keeps the vendor's one-tile ring
of floor tiles, sends one crate per interval to a free tile, and waits until one frees when they are all occupied. The
candidate tiles are computed once at acceptance and only classified on delivery, so it costs no zone scan.

Zones are capped at 256 tiles, 16 per grid and 256 per round, and a buyer at 10 live orders, so marker spam and order
floods can't grow without bound. Closing a zone stops its orders at once and refunds the crates that never arrived; a
refund the buyer's body can't take is kept against their player account and paid when they next spawn or their bank
accepts it.
