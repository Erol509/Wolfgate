# MeridianTraits

Character traits ported from Meridian Rift's quirks, picked in the character editor like any other trait. This first
set only covers quirks that run on components Wolfgate already has: Deaf, Pseudobulbar Affect, Illuminated, Fragility,
Rad Fiend, Hardened Soles and the Canine and Avian speech traits.

Entry points: the trait prototypes in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/MeridianTraits/traits.yml`, and
`WFPermanentDeafSystem`, which puts `DeafComponent` back after a knockout or a deaf status removes it.

Fragility and Rad Fiend each add a `DamageProtectionBuff`, as Dermal Armor and Hardened Lymphocytes do. A mob only
holds one, so these four are mutually exclusive.

## Hemophagia

Meridian's Hemophagia quirk, as the `WFHemophagia` trait (and `WFHemophagiaMasquerade`, which also lets the tumor
keep ordinary food down). On spawn `WFHemophageSystem` swaps the heart for `WFOrganHemophageTumor`, which can't be
surgically removed: the condition is permanent. Wolfgate bodies have no tongue, so the trait also gives the head a
tongue slot holding `WFOrganHemophageTongue`. While the tumor is in a body, the body carries `WFHemophageComponent`:
no hunger, thirst or breathing (`BreathingImmunity`), no natural blood regeneration (marked in `BloodstreamSystem`),
and wound bleeding at 1.2x (marked in `WoundBleedingSystem`). The tumor corrupts the liver, lungs, stomach and tongue
(`WFTumorCorruptionSystem`), at once on spawn and 60 s after any later transplant; a corrupted organ in a body with no
tumor withers.

Blood is one pool of up to 1000/560 of a normal bloodstream: the bloodstream stays its usual size so Wolfmed reads a
full one, and `WFHemophageComponent.Reserve` holds the rest and tops it up. The tumor uses blood all the time (not in
a closet), and the host dies when the pool falls to a fifth. Blood drunk or injected goes straight into the pool, and
so do blood packs (`HealingSystem.Hemophage.cs`, marked in `HealingSystem`) and an IV drip (`WolfmedIvDripSystem`
asks `BloodRoom` and `TryGiveBlood`). The blood gauge alert stands in for Meridian's status tab line; clicking it
gives the exact amount. Mail sometimes brings a hemophage four blood packs (`WFMailGoodiesEvent`, marked in the DeltaV
`MailSystem`), as Meridian's quirk mail goodie does.

Actions: Drain Victim, granted by a corrupted tongue (`WFHemophageDrainerComponent`), so it goes with the tongue: a
3 s bite from a living body being pulled, on a 10 s cooldown so the victim gets a window to break free, and refused
for players who turn off the `WFHemophageDrain` consent toggle. During the bite the victim can't move, break free, interact, attack, shoot or
throw (`WFBeingDrainedSystem`, shared so the victim's client predicts it), refused for chaplains, garlic eaters and blood that isn't blood (sap,
slime, sugar, a machine's fluids); animal blood (monkeys and kobolds included) and other species' blood
(`ForeignBloods`: ammonia, copper, insect and the like) is foul, caps at normal, never satisfies and sickens; a player's blood is worth more and satiates the tumor for 30 minutes. Dormant State (a tenth
of the blood use, but slow, three times the brute, burn and toxin damage, 1.5 times the stamina damage through a
marked read-back in `StaminaSystem`, and no other powers), Hemokinetic Regeneration, Hemokinetic Clot (the clot
reopens when the limb is hurt) and Master of the House (breathe again, half the stamina, slowly regain blood; ending
it short of breath hands the lungs back over 40 s).

A corrupted stomach throws up any food not tagged `WFBloodyFood` (or the blood tomato), and a corrupted liver any
drink under 75% blood, iron, Bloody Mary, Bloodshot or Taste Suppressor (`WFDrankEvent`, marked in `DrinkSystem`;
pills and smoke are left alone). Dormancy, the masquerade variant and the Taste Suppressor reagent stop that. The
blood dishes, Bloodshot and Taste Suppressor are in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/MeridianTraits/hemophage_food.yml`.

Meridian's disgust is stood in for by `WFHemophageComponent.Nausea` (`WFHemophageSystem.Nausea.cs`), limited to what
the tumor finds vile: animal blood and rejected food raise it to 40, a player's blood settles it, and it fades by 0.5 a
second. From 25 it brings stutters and shakes, at Meridian's rates; like Meridian's, these sources stop at 40, short
of the retching. Wolfgate has no
moods, diseases, blood types, lunchbox or Genetic Freak quirk, so Meridian's hooks into those have nothing to attach
to. Peanut butter in the blood cake is sugar here.
