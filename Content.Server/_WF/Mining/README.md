# Mining

Paces the material economy so the faction voucher ladder takes about 150 minutes to climb.

- Ores: gold, silver, plasma and uranium veins drop single ores, about two thirds of upstream. Lithium drops 1-2 per
  vein and is the main tuning knob. Sand and chromite rocks spawn in the 9 km ring at half their old weight and in
  the Wilds; andesite leaves the ring.
- Smelting: raw iron, quartz, gold, silver, plasma, uranium and bananium ore are worth 60% of what they used to be, so
  smelting them pays 1.3 to 1.7 times the ore. Refined materials keep their price, which item, wreck and ship values
  are built from. `SmeltingValueTest` keeps every ore-smelting recipe at least 1.2 times its input value.
- Scrap: the scrap processor smelts strategic sheets at 250 raw scrap (lithium 1,000) with the upgrade discount, and
  diamond and bluespace at 16,000 without it. Lathe upgrade kits don't fit ore processors, except the hyper-convection kit. Plastitanium is uncommon
  in scrap rolls and drone loot, and the crystallizer makes five plastitanium (`WFSheetPlastitanium5`) per batch from
  far more gas; plasteel and plasma also cost more gas.
- Research: the capital ships (Luda, Flyssa, Saturn) sit behind `WFTsfCapitalVouchers` and `WFPdvCapitalVouchers`,
  so each faction's top tier costs 585k research points. Research datafarm boards take 8 research disks.
- Hokkaido: the gas sale console pays MMC industrial credits. Fissile uranium sells for 10,000 per sheet instead of
  25,000.

`GasDepositSystem.Wolfgate.cs` handles gas sales: a gas sale console that pays another currency appraises in it
("N MIC"), and a sale that would pay nothing is refused, leaving the gas in the sale points. `MarketModifier`'s
`hideExamine` drops the rate line from examine, since the Hokkaido console states its rate in its description.

`GatherableSystem.WFGatherSound.cs` plays one rock-break sound per 0.2 s within 3 tiles, so mass mining makes one
networked audio entity per cluster instead of one per rock. `GatherSoundThrottleTest` covers it.
