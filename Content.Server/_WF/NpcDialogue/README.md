# NpcDialogue

Encounter ships talk by how the fight is going instead of repeating one threat. Each ship rates itself every second
(`WFNpcDialogueSystem.Assess`): hull left above the wreck line, crew aboard, working guns, and the strength of the
vessel it is fighting give a mood of Confident, Wary, Hurt, Desperate or Broken. In a fight the mood only gets worse;
five quiet minutes reset it.

- **Lines by mood.** Zone warnings and attack calls pick `{zoneLines}-warn-{mood}`, `-attack-hurt` and so on, falling
  back on the common `wf-npc-voice-*` lines in `Resources/Locale/en-US/_WF/NpcDialogue`. A hurt ship never falls
  back on a confident threat.
- **Talk only when something changes.** A side warns a vessel once, gives a final warning 30 s later, then says
  nothing until the vessel leaves and comes back. A ship speaks of its mood only when it gets worse, at most every 40 s.
- **Memory.** Each side keeps a `WFTalkState` per vessel on the encounter: it brings up once that the vessel came back,
  ran from an earlier fight, fired first, or knocked out one of its ships, and gloats once when the vessel is hurt.
- **Targeted radio.** `WFNpcRadioSystem` sends zone lines only to radios near the speaker and its target, drops a second
  crew talking over the first on a channel, and caps routine traffic (radio officer docking and jump calls). Maydays
  always go out.
- **Mood drives behaviour.** A Hurt extorter makes no new demands. A Desperate ship picks no new fights and, if its
  voice retreats, its pilots run from attackers instead of circling them.
- **Surrender** (`WFShipSurrenderSystem`). A Broken ship fighting a player vessel rolls once against its voice's
  `surrender` chance. On a success its crew stand down (pacified, gunners off the guns), it releases any clamps, holds
  still, counts as out of the fight so its encounter resolves and pays, and hails the victor to accept or refuse.
  Refused, or hit by a player ship more than 10 s after giving up, it fights to the end and never offers again.

Voices (`wfNpcVoice` in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/NpcDialogue/voices.yml`) set each faction's surrender chance and
whether it runs; a ship whose zone lines have no voice surrenders at 10% and runs.
