# BoardingAction

Ways to pin a ship down, on top of the rule that any powered console within 1536 m blocks a jump.

Entry points: `WFFtlDisruptionSystem` (the block itself), `WFHackHarpoonSystem` (the hack) and `WFBoardingHelmSystem`
(the helm banner and its release button).

## FTL block

A held ship carries `WFFtlDisruptedComponent`, with one entry for each thing holding it. `CanFTL` refuses it through
`ConsoleFTLAttemptEvent`, `TrySetupFTL` refuses it whatever started the jump, the expedition console refuses it before
generating a mission, and a docked group can't leave while any ship in it is held. A spool already running is stopped
(`ShuttleSystem.WfAbortFtlSpool`).

Stations, protected grids and ships docked to them can't be tackled (`IsTackleable`), and a hacked ship that docks to
one slips the hack.

## Hacking harpoon

The gun and its darts aren't sold to the public. PDV buys them at its requisition station with Doubloons, the
Underground at `UndergroundVend` with Telecrystals, and Viper Group researches `WFViperGroupHackHarpoon` and prints them
at the Viper lathe. The listings, tech and recipes are in `hack_harpoon_sources.yml`.

`WFHackHarpoonGun` is a universal gun for any hardpoint of Light size or larger, fired from gunnery or the cockpit and
built on the Tether module's harpoon (`ShipHarpoonTurret` with `manned: false`). It is loaded by hand with one
`WFHackDart`. A dart that glances, misses or
meets a shield is destroyed, and a hit has to be square on, as with any harpoon.

The cable (`WFRopeHackCable`) pulls on nothing and has sparks running along it. It pays out as the ships move, keeping
a little slack, and parts at 250 m. On the latch:

- The target's FTL drops at once, its helms sound an alarm and show a warning, and blue arcs run across all its
  engines until the last cable is gone, with bursts of sparks.
- After 8 seconds it is locked down: its dampening is clamped at four times park, and no pilot, autopilot or crew can
  change that (`ShuttleSystem.WfSetLockdownDamping`). Its guns won't fire along any line that crosses the hacker's hull, its
  seekers won't take the hacker, and its guided missiles won't steer onto it. Only the ship the gun is on is protected,
  not what is docked to it.
- For as long as the cable is out, no ship gun on the hacker or on a ship docked to it can fire, flares included. A
  station doesn't pass this on to the other ships docked at it. Those ships' helms show a Release button, and a gunner
  who fires is told why nothing happens.

The clamp only raises damping, so a locked-down ship can still crawl, and it is left in park when the lockdown ends. Its
crew can end the hack by cutting the cable or prying the
dart out of the hull. It also ends if the cable parts, either ship jumps, the gun is destroyed, the dart comes out, or
the wall or machine holding the dart is destroyed, which takes the dart with it. A hack has no time limit unless
`maxDuration` is set on the gun.

Both gun rules are one handler on `ShotAttemptedEvent` for `FireControllable` guns, which every gunnery, cockpit, NPC and
signal shot passes through.

## Ion disruptor

`WFIonDisruptor` (SHRIKE) is a Light universal gun that fires `WFIonDisruptorBolt` every 8 s, reaching about 950 m. A
bolt that hits a hull (`WFEngineDisruptorSystem`) EMPs every thruster and gyroscope on it for 20 s, so the ship drifts
dead, and the helms get a warning popup. Shields stop it, and stations and protected grids shrug it off. It isn't sold:
the Reaver abductors and extorters carry it (see Encounters), with `WFHackHarpoonGunRaider`, an ARACHNE whose dart
reloads itself.
