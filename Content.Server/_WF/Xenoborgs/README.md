# Xenoborgs

Wolfgate's setup for Mono's xenoborg content (ported with Monolith #4558): the `MonoXeno` game preset, the schedulers
that send xenoborg shuttle events (`MonoXenoborgShuttleSpawnerScheduler` and a slowed variant), and the prototypes
around them: tiles, walls, a borg module, a jetpack, a lathe recipe for the basic tool module (added to Mono's
xenoborg pack), crate fills and access names. The xenoborg mobs, gear and ship maps stay in `_Mono`.

It also ports wizden's xenoborg upgrade modules onto Mono's chassis. The mothership core prints them from its
upgrade pack. Engineer: construction, deflector field, tile gun, nanite gun, oil gun, zap. Heavy: laser, burst fire
laser, guided missile launcher, demolition rockets, radioactive aura. Scout: jump, knife, energy dagger, bio knife,
space movement. A chassis holds one primary weapon module, one secondary weapon module and one sprite-overlay module
(`BorgModuleWhitelist`, checked by `BorgModuleExtrasSystem`). Some modules grant components to the chassis instead of
items (`ComponentBorgModule`). Xenoborgs resist EMPs, and the heavy and scout are flash immune.

Wizden's systems behind these sit at their wizden paths, as whole-file blocks or as `SharedGunSystem` partials here:
self-refilling ballistic ammo, the target finder and guided missiles, per-target projectile damage, hitscan
ignition, the sprite ammo counter, the tile loader, sine wave sprite animation, sprite overlays, the bio knife's
metabolizer bonus, shock delay and the jump ability's own action.

Xenoborg crystals are a second currency at the mothership core. The core prints a xenoborg extractor flatpack
(`XenoborgExtractorSystem`). The extractor only anchors on a station's grid, which here includes player ships. It
drains that grid's HV power and grows a crystal every 15 seconds at full draw. The core takes the crystals for
shells (10 crystals) and every weapon module (1 to 4), including Mono's defense, laser cannon and hypo modules; utility
modules cost materials only, so each module has one recipe. Crystals sell for nothing, so the extractor can't turn
power into money.

The mothership core's AI has a Xenoborg cameras action (`WFActionXenoborgCameras`) that opens a camera monitor on
its xenoborgs, which are cameras already. The core routes their camera subnet itself, so this works on every xenoborg
ship, not only the mothership with its router; the feed shows one xenoborg at a time.

The xenoborg plating floors are recoloured cobalt blue; the circuit floor uses the old circuit tile's royal blue.
Its traces glow and breathe between deep and pale royal blue
as a wave across the floor, and light whatever stands on them: a `wfTileGlow` prototype names the tile, its trace
texture and colours, and the client's `WFTileGlowOverlay` draws it, since the engine can't animate or light tiles.

The xenoborg walls (`WallXenoborg`, `WallNanolaminateXorg` and their diagonals) keep their panel frames, with the
carved panels in a muted royal blue and the nanolaminate wall's neon band along their open edges in the circuit floor's royal blue.

Not ported: wizden's xenoborg game mode and its borg-bodied mothership core (Mono's core stays a playable station AI
with a lathe), and the
keyed triggers that make missiles explode at the end of their flight (here they despawn).
