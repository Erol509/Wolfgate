# Census

Questions staff put to every player once, set up from the SSymphony panel's census page. A census has up to 30
questions, each single choice, multiple choice or text. While one is open, players answer it from **Census** in the
lobby's footer and in the game menu (Esc); the button shows how many they have yet to answer, as `Census (2)`. A
player in the lobby has the window opened for them once per census and connection, on reaching the lobby or when one
opens; a player in a round gets a chat line instead and is never interrupted. Each player answers each census once.

Players only ever get the open censuses and whether they answered each: never other answers or counts. The server
checks every answer against the questions; text answers lose control characters, are trimmed and cut to length.

Entry points: `CensusSystem` (server: the open censuses, prompts, answers, closing at `closes_at`),
`CensusQuestions` (reading, normalising and checking the JSON), `CensusUIController`, `CensusWindow` and
`CensusButton` (client), `CensusRules` (limits) and `SymphonyCensusSystem` (the panel's endpoints, in the Symphony
module).

CVar: `wf.census.enabled` (replicated). Off hides the buttons, stops prompts and refuses answers; the panel's
endpoints still work, so staff can prepare a census.

## For the panel

`symphony_module` in `/status` is 16 or more on a build with this; the panel can also probe `GET /symphony/census`
(404 on a build without it).

### Questions

```json
[{"id":"q1","kind":"single","prompt":"Favourite map?","options":["Frontier","Coda"],"required":true},
 {"id":"q2","kind":"multi","prompt":"Which do you play?","options":["Mining","Salvage","Trade"],"required":false,"min":1,"max":2},
 {"id":"q3","kind":"text","prompt":"Anything else?","required":false,"maxLength":500,"multiline":true}]
```

1 to 30 questions, at most 64 KiB. `id` is 1 to 24 of `a-z0-9_`, unique; `prompt` 1 to 300 characters; `options`
2 to 20 distinct choices of 1 to 100 characters. Multi: `min` (default 0, or 1 when required) and `max` (default
the number of options), `0 <= min <= max <= options`, and `max` at least 1. Text: `maxLength` 1 to 2000 (default
500), `multiline` (default false). Unknown keys are dropped.

### Endpoints

With the admin API token (`Authorization: SS14Token <admin.api_token>`). Refusals are
`{"error": "<code>", "message": "<a sentence for staff>"}`.

- `GET /symphony/census`: `{"symphony_module", "censuses": [census...]}`, newest first. A census is `id`, `title`,
  `description`, `questions` (the array), `state` (counting an elapsed `closes_at`), `anonymous`, `created_by`,
  `created_at`, `opened_at`, `closes_at`, `closed_at` (ISO 8601 or null) and `responses` (how many answered).
- `POST /symphony/census` with `{"id": null, "title", "description", "questions", "anonymous", "closes_at", "state",
  "actor"}` creates one; with an id it updates that one. Answers the census. `bad_request` (400: not JSON, or no
  title, questions or state), `invalid` (400: a field breaks a rule, or `closes_at` is past while `state` is open),
  `not_found` (404), `has_responses` (409: questions or anonymous changed once anyone has answered; compared after
  normalising). A closed census can be reopened. When a census goes open, players online who have not answered it
  are told.
- `POST /symphony/census/delete` with `{"id", "actor"}` deletes it and its answers: `{"symphony_module", "id",
  "deleted": true}`, or `not_found`.
