# TestDummies

An admin tool that asks players whether they will be tested on. Open it with `testdummies` or the Test Dummies
button on the Wolfgate tab of the admin menu. The admin types what is being tested (optional), how long the prompt
stays up, and who to ask: players who own a ship (a deed they carry, or a ship registered to their account), who are
in sector space (not on a planet, in FTL, on an expedition or inside a vehicle), who are playing a body, and who are
not admins. Every ticked condition must hold. Asking sends each matching player a chime and a prompt in the bottom
right of their screen with the description, a disclaimer that no refunds or compensation will be given, a countdown,
and Yes/No buttons.

The window lists everyone asked, opted-in first, with their character, ship and whether they are still connected, and
updates as answers come in. Each answer is also posted to admin chat. End call takes down the prompts nobody answered;
a new call replaces the list. `testdummies list` prints the same list in the console. One call runs at a time, and the
round end clears it.

Entry points: `WFTestDummyCommand`, `WFTestDummyEui` and `WFTestDummySystem` (audience matching, sending and the list)
on the server; `WFTestDummyWindow` and `WFTestDummyPromptSystem` (draws the `WFTestDummyChip`) on the client.
