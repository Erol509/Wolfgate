# CrewBarks

Mother Base manners for a commanded ship's NPC crew and counter staff, in the spirit of Metal Gear Solid V: they
take a punch from the Boss with thanks instead of fighting back, salute and greet the Boss and the staff as they pass,
are cooler with strangers and curt with the barred, think aloud now and then, and react to what happens aboard. A ship
opts in with `WFCrewBarksComponent` on its grid (the Lupercal does, in its vessel's `addComponents`).

## Who is the Boss

ShipCommand's `IsBoss`: the owner, the officers, and admins while the crew let admins be (`AdminsNeverTargeted`) or
count them as officers (`AdminsInZones`), as `Welcome` does. ShipCommand answers NpcCrew's `WFCrewForgiveEvent` for
them on every commanded ship, barking or not: a blow, shove or pickpocketing from the Boss is not remembered, not
answered by the crew, not put on the radio and leaves the Boss welcome. NpcCrew asks at each place a blow makes an
enemy (`WFCrewSystem`'s damage and strip hooks, the radio officer's Mayday, and upstream `NPCRetaliationSystem.TryRetaliate`
through one marked line). Anyone else's blow is answered as before.

## Voices and lines

Every roster member on a barking ship gets `WFCrewBarkerComponent` when the roster staffs his post
(`WFShipPostStaffedEvent`), or when the ship starts barking. His voice is the grid's `voices` entry for his entity
prototype or role, else his role's name without `WFCrew` (`marine`), `radio` for the radio operator, or `trader` for
counter staff. A line of a kind (`WFCrewBark`) is a random one of `wf-crew-bark-<voice>-<kind>-<n>` and
`wf-crew-bark-common-<kind>-<n>`, never the same as his last; the system counts each pool up from 1, so adding a line
is a Fluent edit only (`Resources/Locale/en-US/_WF/CrewBarks`). Lines are said aloud through the normal chat.

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| `struck` / `attacked` | the Boss / anyone else hits or shoves him |
| `greet-boss` (with a salute), `greet-staff`, `greet-guest`, `greet-barred` | someone passes within `GreetRange` in sight |
| `idle` | every `IdleMin`–`IdleMax`, with a player within `HearRange` |
| `healed` | an NPC medic treats him, or he is healed `HealedAt` or more at once |
| `relieved` (`$name`) / `stood-down` | a player takes his job / the bridge stands his post down; replaces the roster's line |
| `reporting` | restaffed at the crew bay, he reaches his post |
| `fight` | his crew raise an alert on a hostile he can see |
| `jump` / `arrived` | the ship starts / ends an FTL jump; someone near each player says it |

## Limits

`WFCrewBarksComponent` holds the tuning. A crewman says one line per `LineGap` (20 s); a thank-you for the Boss's blow
has its own `StruckGap` (3 s) and skips the area limit, and relief lines skip both. Any other line keeps everyone within
`AreaRange` (6 m) quiet for `AreaGap` (6 s). He greets each person once per `GreetAgain` (5 min), a guest only at
`GuestGreetChance`, nobody he is fighting and no crewman's unwelcome visitor (the zones' warnings speak for them); the
sweep runs once a second. A crewman in a fight of his own neither greets nor idles. `WFCrewBarkerComponent` keeps his
last line and a count per kind, for admins and the tests.
