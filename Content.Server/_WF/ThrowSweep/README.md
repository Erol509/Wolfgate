# ThrowSweep

Fast thrown things and people no longer pass through walls. The engine has no continuous collision: it only tests
where a body ends each physics step, so anything covering more than about a wall's width in one step lands on the far
side. At `physics.target_minimum_tickrate` 30 a step is a whole tick, so 30 m/s is already a metre a step.

- `WFThrowSweepSystem` notes, before each physics step, where every thrown thing faster than `wf.throw_sweep_speed`
  (6 m/s) is. After the step, if its centre crossed the face of a static hard body (wall, window, closed door) on the
  way, it is put back just in front of it and bounces off at a tenth of its speed. Anything the physics step would let
  through (`PreventCollideEvent`) is let through here too. Slower throws hit walls as ordinary collisions. Server-only.
- Ship collisions threw everything loose aboard at the ship's whole change of speed, with no cap. Both impact throws
  are now capped at `wf.impact_throw_max` (12 m/s, and 12 m).
