# Pool

Playable 8-ball on the pool and billiard tables (`CoyoteTableSnooker` and its five other pieces,
`DarkPackTableWoodBilliard`, `RMCTablePoolTableBlack`, `N14TableCasinoPool`). Using a table, or clicking it with a pool
cue, opens the game window. A player joins one of the two sides and, with a pool cue (`WFPoolCue`, crafted from two
wood planks or bought from the Wolfgate vendor) in a hand, shoots on their turn: the mouse aims, holding the button
and pulling back sets the power, letting go shoots. With nobody on the other side one player shoots for both. Anyone
else at the table can open the window and watch. Leaving the table gives up the side; the game stays on the table.

Rules: solids against stripes, set by the first ball pocketed after the break. A scratch, hitting nothing or hitting
the wrong ball first gives the other side ball in hand. Pocketing the 8 last wins; pocketing it early, or with a foul,
loses; on the break it goes back on its spot. The winner breaks the next rack.

Entry points: `PoolSystem` (server: window, sides, shots), `PoolGame` (rules), `PoolSim` (ball physics) and
`PoolRules` (every number) in `Content.Shared/_WF/Pool`, and the client's `PoolWindow` and `PoolTableControl`.

The server plays each shot to its end at once and sends the table before the shot, the cue ball's velocity and the
table after it. Clients replay the shot with the same `PoolSim` and then settle on the server's table, and the server
takes no new shot until the balls would have stopped. A table built from several pieces keeps its game on one of
them (`WFPoolTableSystem.GetRoot`), and its window stays open for anyone within `range` of any piece.
