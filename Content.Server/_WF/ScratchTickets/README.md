# ScratchTickets

Scratch tickets in three tiers. A ticket hides its symbols under foil; the holder uses it in hand and drags across the
foil to wear it away, and once 90% is off the rest goes and the ticket is scratched. A match wins the prize printed in
the ticket's legend, and each tier matches its own way (`WFScratchFormat`). Using a scratched winner on any powered bank ATM pays the prize into the user's account (as
cash for an Ironman or anyone without an account) and takes the ticket.

| Ticket | Price | Wins | Prizes | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mother Lode (`WFScratchTicket`) | 500 | 35% | 250 to 50,000 | `MatchThree`: three alike in a 3 by 3 grid |
| Foundry Gold (`WFScratchTicketFoundry`) | 2,500 | 22% | 2,500 to 500,000 | `WinningSymbol`: find the winning symbol among six |
| Thaler Tycoon (`WFScratchTicketTycoon`) | 10,000 | 12% | 25,000 to 5,000,000 | `Pairs`: three games of two, both alike |

Unscratched tickets stack to 20 and show as a banded pile. Using a pile in hand pulls one ticket into a free hand and
opens it. Each ticket is rolled when it leaves the pile, and one that has been opened no longer stacks, so a known
ticket can't be swapped for a fresh one.

Tickets turn up in maintenance loot, the dearer ones less often, and Scratches-to-Win sells all three, singly and in
packs of 10 and 20, at the card table in the Caelestinus Central bar lounge. He is an ordinary trader (`Traders`), so payment is cash or an ID on his table.

Entry points: `WFScratchTicketSystem` (roll at map init, pulling from a pile, reveal, ATM payout), `WFScratchTicketComponent` (symbols,
weights and the rolled cells), `WFScratchTicketRules` (the formats and the roll), `WFScratchTicketWindow` and `WFScratchFoil` (the
card and its foil).

The outcome is rolled on the server when the ticket spawns and only reaches the client that opens it. The scratching
itself is client-side: the server takes the client's word that the foil is off, which decides nothing. Half-scratched
foil is kept on the client (`WFScratchFoilComponent`) while the ticket stays in view.

Odds, prizes, format, wording and card colours are on each ticket's prototype (`ticket.yml`): weights out of 10000, and every
tier returns about 65% of its price on average. `WFScratchTicketTest` holds each tier to a 50 to 80% return, fewer
wins than the tier below and a bigger top prize.
