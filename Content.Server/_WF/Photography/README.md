# Photography

Instant cameras, film, photos, photo albums and picture frames. Using a camera on a spot prints a photo of the square
around it, which can be named, described and written on, kept in an album or hung in a frame. A character with the
Photographer trait selected spawns with a personal album that is saved through the round, wherever it is, and comes
back when that character spawns again. Albums can't be bought or printed. Station AIs and the cyborg
photography module keep photos in a gallery and print them on demand. Holy water turns a camera into a camera
obscura, which photographs ghosts. The mechanics are inspired by tgstation's photography, and the sprites and sounds
are taken from tgstation.

Entry points: `PhotoCameraSystem` (taking a photo), the client's `PhotoCaptureSystem` (rendering it), `PhotoStoreSystem`
(images by hash, kept in the database), `PhotoAlbumSystem` (saving and restoring personal albums), `PhotoSystem`
(labels and back text), `PhotoGallerySystem`, `PhotoFrameSystem` and `PhotographerSystem` (the Photographer trait).
Server settings are in `PhotoCVars` (`wf.photo.*`).

Photos are rendered by the photographer's client, so the server can't vouch for what they show. Moderators remove one
with `blankphoto <hash or photo/frame entity>`: it deletes the image and blanks every copy, in the round and in saved
albums when they next load. Each photo's hash and author are in the `WFPhoto` admin logs.
