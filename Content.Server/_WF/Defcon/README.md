# Defcon

A sector-wide DEFCON level, from 5 (FADE OUT) to 1 (COCKED PISTOL), that follows what is happening in the sector.
It informs and alerts only; no gameplay changes with the level.

Every 30 seconds `WFDefconSystem` raises `WFDefconGatherEvent` and adds up what the sources report.
`WFDefconSourcesSystem` scores the ADS and Reaver campaigns by tier, the war level while HOT (which also holds
DEFCON at 3 or worse), open scheduled threat encounters and distress calls, and player deaths in the last ten
minutes. Another module can add a source by subscribing to the event; Ordnance scores nuclear devices held
(`nuclearHeld`, holding DEFCON at `nuclearHeldFloor`) and armed (`nuclearArmed`, holding it at 1). A higher level is reached once the score has
held for a minute, and it may skip levels. A lower level comes one step at a time, after ten minutes below and at
least five minutes at the current level. All of it is tuned in the `wfDefconProfile` prototype that
`wf.defcon.profile` names (`WFDefault`); `wf.defcon.enabled` turns it off.

Each change goes out through the `Announcements` module: a banner (with the top reasons on a rise) and a chip. Every
change plays the profile's `alarm`, then the level's `callout` once the alarm ends; a level's `siren` (DEFCON 1's)
plays alongside the alarm. PDAs show `DEFCON: n` under the war level. Admins drive it from the Sector DEFCON panel (Wolfgate admin tab, `wf_defconpanel`: set or lock a level, with or
without an announcement, unlock, bias the score, score it now, and see each source's share) or with `wf_defcon`
(`status`, `set`, `lock`, `unlock`, `bias`, `eval`).
