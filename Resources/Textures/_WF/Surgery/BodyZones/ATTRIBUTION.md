# Body zone assets

`base.png` and the limb overlays are extracted from tgstation's `icons/hud/screen_slimecore.dmi`
(`zone_sel`) and `icons/hud/screen_gen.dmi` (the per-zone states), used by the operating computer's body
zone selector.

Source: https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation

for its assets. The files here are unmodified crops of those sprites.
