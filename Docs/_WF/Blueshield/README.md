# Blueshield

Blueshield Officer clothing ported from [GoobStation](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station): uniforms,
armour, the hardsuit, the mantle and the beret, bags, the chest rig, headset, glasses and gas mask. The sprites are
GoobStation's, including the updated beret and mantle artwork.

Vagrants (the Contractor job) can pick the clothing from their existing loadout groups: jumpsuit, outer clothing,
head, neck, shoes, belt, back, ears, eyes and face. The armored beret, armored ushanka, armored winter coat,
armored cowboy jacket and hardsuit stay out of the Vagrant loadout.

Two GoobStation pieces are left out: the BSO envirosuit and envirohelm need the Plasmaman species, which Wolfgate
does not have, and the filled chest rig holds reagents Wolfgate does not have. The BSO ensemble switches between
its black and white cloak, mantle and cloak-and-mantle forms through the ItemSwitch module.
