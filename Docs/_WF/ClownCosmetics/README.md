# ClownCosmetics

Clown and mime facepaints, clothing, EVA suits and toys ported from other forks. Players pick them in the
'Contractor' loadout, which multiple roles inherit: facepaints under Head markings, toys under Trinkets (packed in
the backpack) and the clown modsuit under Backpack. The clown battlesuit is spawn-only.

The facepaints are from [Frontier #3841](https://github.com/new-frontiers-14/frontier-station-14/pull/3841) by
Chedd-Error, with sprites by Zergologist. The rest is from Impstation, Starlight, Coyote Sector, ADT, Delta-V,
Moffstation, S.P.L.U.R.T, NovaSector, Floof, tgstation and Space Station 14. Each RSI's `meta.json` and each
`attributions.yml` keeps its source's credit and licence.

Differences from the sources: the facepaints have no species restriction, and the reptilian serious mime facepaint,
which Frontier never defined, is added. Sprite states for species Wolfgate doesn't have are left out. The clown nose
is a breath mask, the clown shoes use the Waddle module's toggle, the security mime mask isn't contraband, the
Coyote outfits sit on `ClothingUniformSkirtBase`, and the unicycle handles like Frontier's.
