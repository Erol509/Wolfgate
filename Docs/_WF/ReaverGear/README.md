# ReaverGear

The Ashfall Reavers' kit: a combat hardsuit and helmet, a void hardsuit, fatigues, deck and engine jumpsuits, a pack,
a chest rig, a headset, a beret and the captain's and warlord's cloaks. The story is stolen TSF M86 gear repainted in
ash orange; the sprites are Lonestar's orange recolour of Monolith's blue TSFMC M86 line, ported by way of
Panta-Rhei PR 1177 (Floof-Station/Panta-Rhei), where they were the Solarian Alliance's. The port's duplicate "blue"
beret was the same brown beret twice and was dropped.

Nothing spawns these for players yet; they are admin-spawnable and worn by the Reavers' NPC crews: bruisers wear the
combat hardsuit and officers the captain's cloak (`_WF/NpcCrew/profiles.yml`). The Reavers' red raider hardsuit
(`WFClothingOuterHardsuitReaver`, the death squad RSI Wolfgate already ships) lives with the crew gear in the NpcCrew
module and goes on the riflemen.

Component differences from the port: `StaminaResistance` became `StaminaDamageResistance`, the helmet attaches through
Monolith's `clothingPrototypes` map, night vision uses Monolith's M86 helmet settings, `DamageOnShootProtection` and
the `HidesHarpyWings` tag (the hardsuit base already hides wings) were dropped, and the headset carries only the common
and traffic keys. The helmet RSI gained the `on-equipped-HELMET*` states the light prefix needs, and its light layers
were trimmed to the lit pixels.
