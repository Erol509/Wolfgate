# Resprites

Ports visual updates and matching prototypes for closets, lockers, wardrobes, wall-mounted storage, suit storage, and Gray/White/Dark floors, alongside Impstation structure sprites. Closet and floor prototype mappings live under `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Structures/Storage/Closets/` and `Resources/Prototypes/Tiles/`.

GoobStation's command cloak, mantle and quartermaster beret resprites are here too. The chief engineer's old cloak and mantle became the white variants, with a green cloak and an orange mantle added beside them; `Resources/migration.yml` maps the old ids. Prototypes live in `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Clothing/Neck/` and `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Clothing/Head/hats.yml`. The resprited head of security cloak and mantle, and the chief medical officer's cloak and mantle, are offered in the Vagrant neck group.

The chief engineer's white and green ensembles and the chief medical officer's ensemble are ported with the ItemSwitch module, so they switch between cloak, mantle and combined forms.

Funky Station's later resprite batches add ID cards, PDAs, mail, food, tools, chemistry glassware, ammunition, medicine and more. Textures live under `Resources/Textures/_WG/Resprites/Funkystation`, with `.../Wizden` for vanilla-path RSIs and `.../Impstation` or `.../RMC14` for those forks. Only the files a PR changed are copied; unchanged states keep Wolfgate's existing art, and fork-prefixed base RSIs such as `_NF` PDAs and `_Mono` tools are not redirected because those PRs did not touch them.

`Resources/Maps/_WG/Resprites/resprite_test.yml` is a showcase map of the prototypes that use these sprites, with a `resprites` warp point. `TestMap.md` lists every item and its position.
